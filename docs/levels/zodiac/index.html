Why it’s here

An epic, sprawling odyssey.

Zodiac feels like an epic, sprawling odyssey. As a former Top 1 Demon, BIANOX and the team created a monumental three-minute journey set to Creo's “Sphere.” It is heavily segmented, with each creator bringing a distinct astrological theme and design to their part, yet the transitions are smooth enough that it feels like one massive, cohesive gauntlet.

The sheer intimidation factor of having to clear legendary choke points — like Enlex's infamous part, or Xander's brutal finale — makes passing Zodiac a true rite of passage for top players.
