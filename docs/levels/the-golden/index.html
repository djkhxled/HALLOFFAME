Why it’s here

A masterclass in atmospheric pressure.

The Golden is a masterclass in atmospheric pressure. Hosted by Bo and serving as a spiritual successor to El Dorado, it ditches the game's standard colour palettes for a suffocating, deeply overgrown jungle aesthetic of dark greens and golds.

The level is notoriously difficult, particularly in its completely unforgiving wave sections, and the visual clutter deliberately plays tricks on your eyes. When nSwish finally verified it, he conquered a level that felt less like a GD layout and more like a hostile, living environment.
