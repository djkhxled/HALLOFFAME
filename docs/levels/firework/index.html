Why it’s here

A hauntingly beautiful, rhythmic nightmare.

Firework is a hauntingly beautiful, rhythmic nightmare. It was locked in a legendary battle with Slaughterhouse for the Top 1 spot. Instead of typical extreme demon colours, Firework utilises a stunning, monochromatic greyscale aesthetic that makes it feel like an old, melancholic silent film.

The gameplay is obscenely difficult, packed with terrifyingly precise cube timings and straight-fly sections that demand absolute perfection. Trick's verification of this level solidified it as an elegant, brutally difficult masterpiece.
