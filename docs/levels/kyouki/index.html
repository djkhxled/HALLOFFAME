Why it’s here

One person. 287,445 objects.

Kyouki is the one that makes no sense to me. It is a solo level. One person built 287,445 objects of it and then verified it themself, and the verification came after dying at 97% first, which I genuinely cannot imagine recovering from.

The name means madness and the decoration agrees. It is stacked so densely that it stops reading as individual objects and turns into pure texture — and somehow it never gets muddy. That blue is the best blue in the game.
