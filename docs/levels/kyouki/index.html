Why it’s here

Absolute madness, which is exactly what the name means.

Kyouki is absolute madness — which is incredibly fitting, considering that is exactly what the name translates to in Japanese. The fact that this is a fully solo level, entirely created and verified by Demishio, makes it one of the most legendary achievements in the entire game. It stands proudly as the hardest fully-solo level ever legitimately beaten, proving that a single creator's vision can compete with the game's biggest mega-collabs.

The synchronization with Creo's song “Crazy” is completely off the charts. The level doesn't just match the beat; it perfectly captures the frantic, high-octane energy of the track. The gameplay is blisteringly fast, cycling through different game modes at a breakneck pace while surrounded by blinding, neon designs. Because the portals change so rapidly and the timings are near-impossible, learning this level is an absolute nightmare. Demishio purposefully built the gameplay around his own specific skillset, making it a highly unique challenge.

My favorite part has to be just how overwhelming the whole experience is, and of course the dash orb at the start of the first drop. From the rapid cube section at the start to the triple-speed transitions, it never gives you a chance to breathe. Watching top players tackle it was thrilling, especially when Zoink stepped up to become the very first victor shortly after Demishio's brutal 500,000-attempt verification journey. It is a masterpiece of high-speed perfection.
