Why it’s here

The list gets prettier the further down you go.

Aerial Gleam is a 2.2 level that spends its whole runtime in the air. Endlevel hosted it with Antares, Deadlox and CuLuC, and the decoration leans into altitude rather than into hell — pale light, open space, and a sense of being a long way off the ground.

Doggie verified it, and the level's own description credits him with fixing it too. It sits at #140 on the Demonlist, which is further down than most of this list, and it is here on looks and feel rather than on difficulty.
