Why it’s here

The ultimate realisation of the most famous level in GD history.

Bloodlust is the ultimate realisation of the most famous level in GD history. Manix648 took Riot's Bloodbath and transformed it into a sprawling, terrifying, apocalyptic extension that pushed the game to its limits.

But what truly makes Bloodlust immortal is knobbelboy's legendary verification journey. Taking over 120,000 attempts and suffering an utterly heartbreaking, viral death at 98%, his eventual victory remains one of the most hype moments the community has ever seen. The apocalyptic red-and-black aesthetic has never felt more earned.
