Why it’s here

A colossal fortress you have to dismantle.

Titan Complex is a monumental achievement in megacollab history. Hosted by TCTeam, the level feels like a colossal fortress that you have to slowly dismantle.

It is famous for its incredibly balanced but brutal difficulty, demanding mastery across every single game mode without leaning too heavily on just the wave or ship. The level's industrial, almost sci-fi mechanical aesthetic is flawlessly executed, and conquering it feels like taking down a massive, impenetrable boss.
