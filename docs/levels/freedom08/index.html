Why it’s here

An absolute test of human endurance.

Freedom08 is an absolute test of human endurance and one of the most iconic XL extremes in the game. Hosted by Pennutoh and set to xi's legendary rhythm game anthem “FREEDOM DiVE,” it perfectly translates the song's chaotic 222-BPM energy into Geometry Dash.

The level is a masterclass in Pennutoh's signature flow-based gameplay; once you lock into the rhythm, the inputs feel like a beautifully choreographed, high-speed dance. Surviving over four minutes of relentless gameplay only to face the incredibly nerve-wracking final dual is a terrifying but exhilarating experience that cements this level as a stamina masterpiece.
