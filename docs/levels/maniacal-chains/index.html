Why it’s here

A hostile, mechanical hellscape.

Maniacal Chains brings a terrifying level of aggression to the extreme demon roster. LordDivinity perfectly captured the aesthetic of a hostile, mechanical hellscape, utilising heavy, industrial decoration that makes the environment feel like a trap waiting to spring.

The gameplay is heavy on tight spaces and rigorous wave sections, constantly maintaining a high-pressure environment that refuses to let up. It is a pure, concentrated dose of difficulty wrapped in a menacing visual package.
