Why it’s here

A level designed for two people, beaten by one.

Codependence is a mind-bending anomaly in the Geometry Dash community. Created by TCTeam, it is an incredibly brutal Extreme Demon designed specifically for two players, yet the ultimate flex in the community is to beat it entirely solo.

Controlling two separate icons with two hands through unsymmetrical, intensely difficult gameplay requires an almost inhuman level of brain-splitting coordination. It is a psychological and mechanical puzzle as much as it is a level, standing entirely in a league of its own.
