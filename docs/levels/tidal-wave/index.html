Why it’s here

Fifteen months at number one.

Tidal Wave is the one that felt inevitable. Three straight minutes of wave, 255,394 objects, and a difficulty curve that just does not relent — and then it sat at #1 for fifteen months, longer than any level has managed before or since.

What I love is that it's a Nine Circles level at heart. That format has been done a thousand times, and OniLink took it and made the definitive version. It looks like the ocean and it plays like drowning.
