Why it’s here

The magnum opus of the Geometry Dash editor.

Ocular Miracle is the magnum opus of the Geometry Dash editor. Davphla and the team pushed the game's engine to the absolute breaking point, packing in so much stunning, hyper-detailed art that the level literally had to be split into three separate extremes — Reverie, Faith and Diligence — just so people could play it without their games crashing.

It is a sensory explosion of colour, cinematic transitions and breathtaking effects, serving as a monument to what the community's artists can achieve when they completely ignore technical limitations.
