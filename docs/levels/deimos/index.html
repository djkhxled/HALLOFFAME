Why it’s here

Number one, and it isn't close.

Deimos sits at the top of this list because it never lets up and never loses the plot. Three minutes is an absurd amount of time to hold a level together at this difficulty, and most mega-collabs at this scale end up feeling like a compilation — a dozen creators each doing their own thing, stapled together. Deimos doesn't. It reads as one continuous idea from start to finish.

The Phobos lineage matters too. Following up a level that defined an era is a trap, and it would have been easy to make something louder and call it a sequel. Instead it earned the name.
