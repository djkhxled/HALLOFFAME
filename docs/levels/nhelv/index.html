Why it’s here

The level that made decoration the point.

Nhelv is here for how it looks, and I don't think that needs defending. It took the official music video for Silentroom's track and rebuilt it inside Geometry Dash, and the result still gets used as the benchmark for what level design can be years later.

What gets me is the restraint. It would have been easy to make this a wall of effects. Instead every flash is timed to something, and when the level goes quiet it actually goes quiet. Plenty of levels are harder. Very few are this composed.
