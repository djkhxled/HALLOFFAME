Why it’s here

The coldest thing on this list.

Antarctic Lights is the newest level here by a long way, and it earned the spot immediately. It's a remake of a remake of Nine Circles, which sounds like it should be exhausted by now, and instead it's the sharpest version of that idea anyone has managed.

It's also just beautiful. Most levels that go for ice end up looking like blue plastic; this one actually feels cold. The aurora work is the best atmospheric decoration I've seen in a level.
