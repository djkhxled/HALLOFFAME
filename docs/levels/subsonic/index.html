Why it’s here

Speed pushed to the absolute brink.

Subsonic is an adrenaline rush that honours the legendary Sonic series — Supersonic, Hypersonic — by pushing the speed limits to the absolute brink. Hosted by Viprin and set to Djjaner's “SpeedBreaker,” the level's pacing is its defining feature.

It constantly throws rapid portal transitions and blistering speed changes at you, requiring lightning-fast reflexes and intense muscle memory. The way the megacollab seamlessly stitches together different creators' parts while maintaining that distinct, high-octane Sonic identity makes it a classic extreme demon.
