Why it’s here

They just don't make levels like these anymore.

The Yandere holds a very special place in my heart. The sheer intensity of this level is almost unmatched, and the horror-anime theming is so distinct that it completely separates it from the typical hell-themed extremes of its era. To quote my friend unaura, “They just don't make levels like these anymore,” and that is the absolute truth. It captures a specific era of Geometry Dash history perfectly.

This level does the song so much justice, syncing brutal gameplay spikes with the frantic energy of the music. The standout moment for me is right after Spectrex's part, leading into the end. Spectrex was tasked with building the section right before the final drop, and he completely mastered the art of suspense. His transition of flashing his logo and switching the level text to read “FAR” is one of the coolest, most hype-inducing moments in the entire game despite its simplicity — it is FarDreamer's tag, and it also means you're far into the level, which I think is cool.

What truly immortalized this level for me, though, was watching Dorami verify it. When he had that heartbreaking death at 98% with Topi sitting right there in the room, the entire community felt his pain. It became a massive, unifying moment where everyone was actively cheering for him to cross the finish line. When he finally pulled it off, the catharsis was something incredibly special that very few levels ever manage to achieve.
