Why it’s here

A computer virus turned into an Extreme Demon.

Killbot is what happens when you turn a computer virus into an Extreme Demon. Lithifusion crafted an absolute masterpiece of sensory overload; the level actively tries to blind, confuse and distract you with aggressive screen vomit, flashing warning signs and fake pathways.

Bold Step's verification was legendary because playing Killbot isn't just about mechanical skill — it is an intense memory test where you have to decipher the actual gameplay from a chaotic, glitching screen. It is one of the most uniquely themed levels ever made.
