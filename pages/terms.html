What this is

Baylor's Hall of Extremes is one person's ranked list of twenty-five Geometry Dash extreme demons, with commentary and researched facts about each. It is a hobby project. It sells nothing, hosts no user accounts, accepts no submissions and carries no advertising.

Opinion, not authority

The ranking is a personal favourites list. It is explicitly not a difficulty ranking, and it does not speak for, compete with or claim any relationship to Pointercrate, the AREDL, or any other community list. Where a page states an opinion, it is one person's opinion and is offered as such.

Accuracy

Facts on each level page — creators, verifiers, dates, attempt counts, object counts, songs — are researched and sourced, and every level page lists its sources. Anything that could not be confirmed is shown as an em dash rather than guessed at.

That said, the underlying sources are community-maintained and change over time, and demonlist positions move constantly. Information here may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. It is provided for interest only. If you spot an error, please say so and it will be corrected.
