Artwork on this site

The artwork on every level page is original work made for this site. Nothing here is a screenshot, a frame grab, or a copy of any level's assets. Each piece is an interpretation drawn from scratch — in code, as SVG — in response to a level's colours, motifs and atmosphere, in the same spirit as a poster of a film rather than a still from it.

Where a level's own imagery is well known, the interpretation is deliberately recognisable. That resemblance is the point of a tribute, and it is offered as commentary and criticism. If you built one of these levels and would rather your work were not evoked this way, say so and it will be changed or removed.
